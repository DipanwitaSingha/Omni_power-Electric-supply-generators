## 🚀 Omnipower-Electrical_generators

GeneratorHub is a modern, customer-focused web platform designed to make purchasing, renting, and managing generators easier and more convenient.

The platform allows customers to explore different generators, view product images and working details, place orders, track deliveries, raise queries after delivery, and request Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) services — all through one integrated system.

It combines an e-commerce experience with smart customer support and generator service management.

## ✨ Key Features

### ⚙️ 1. Interactive Generator Showcase
- Explore different generator models.
- View generator images and product details.
- Understand generator components and working functions.
- Access essential product specifications.

### 🛒 2. Generator E-Commerce Platform
- Purchase generators directly through the website.
- Explore available generator models.
- Choose between buying and renting generators.
- Access product-related information before placing an order.

### 📦 3. Smart Order Tracking
- Track orders through the system.
- Monitor order progress and delivery status.
- Access order-related information.
- Stay updated throughout the purchasing process.

### 💬 4. Customer Query & Support System
- Customers can submit queries after their orders have been delivered.
- Provide contact details for customer communication.
- Raise service-related requests and product complaints.
- Improve customer experience through an organized support system.

### 🔧 5. AMC — Annual Maintenance Contract
- AMC service facilities for generator maintenance.
- Customer assistance for maintenance-related requirements.
- Support for long-term generator servicing and maintenance.

### 🔄 6. Generator Rental Services
- Explore generators available for rent.
- Access rental-related product information.
- Provide an additional option for customers who do not want to purchase a generator.
